1. Paraphrase (summarize) the video in a few (1 or 2) lines.
  - The video describes that the single parameter that determines if someone would excel in their respective fields is grit, i.e. the passion and perseverance for very long-term goals. Growth mindset is the way someone can build grit over time, which says that the ability to learn anything is not immutable and that it can change when someone puts enough effort.
    
2. Paraphrase (summarize) the video in a few (1 or 2) lines in your own words.
  - The video explain difference between fixed and growth mindset where fixed mindset believe that learning is not something we can change overtime even if someone puts a lot of effort and hence they believe someone is born with a talent where they excel, whereas people who believe in the growth mindset don't think that learning anything is possible at any stage of life if enough efforts are put into learning and hence they don't shy away from trying out and learning new things.
    
3. What is the Internal Locus of Control? What is the key point in the video?
  - Locus of Control is the extent to which someone has control over their life. Internal Locus of Control says you control what happens in life as opposed to the belief in an External Locus of Control that says external factors decide if you excel at something. Those who believe in Internal Locus of Control stay motivated while those who believe otherwise give up easily as their efforts don't matter against external factors.
    
4. What are the key points mentioned by speaker to build growth mindset (explanation not needed).
  - Believe in your ability to figure things out
  - Question your assumptions
  - Create your own curriculum for growth
  - Honor the struggle
    
5. What are your ideas to take action and build Growth Mindset?
- Own my learning: I take 100% responsibility for my learning and for every project I’m given, from execution to delivery.
- Stay relaxed and focused: I treat new concepts as learning opportunities, not pressure.
- Research first: I use documentation, Google, Stack Overflow and GitHub Issues before asking for help.
- Persist: I stay with a problem until it’s solved, because more effort leads to deeper understanding.
- Understand deeply: I never write code I don’t understand.
- Learn from setbacks: I treat confusion, discomfort and errors as signals of where to learn.
- Finish with quality: My code isn’t done until it works, is readable, is modular and is efficient.
