### 3. What are active and diffused modes of thinking?

- Active mode of thinking is a state of intense attention where one concentrates on familiar patterns or concepts, and the thoughts in the mind move like a pinball bumping into closely packed barriers. Diffused mode of thinking is a relaxed state of mental activity where the brain rests, and it allows one to gain a new perspective on new thoughts; in this case, the thoughts are like a pinball bumping into barriers spaced far apart, allowing thoughts to travel widely and reach new, unexplored areas to grapple with new concepts.
