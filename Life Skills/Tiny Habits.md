### 6. Write about the book's perspective on how to make a habit easier to do?
- There are 4 steps toward building a habit: Cue, Craving, Response and Reward. Cue is the trigger that makes us perform certain task,
  followed by the craving which is the motivation, the response is the action we perform and the reward is the end goal of successful completion
  of that task. Together they work as a psychological mechanism to make it easier for anyone to make it appealing to the brain to easily get into
  a habit.  

### 7. Write about the book's perspective on how to make a habit harder to do?
- To make a habit harder to do, one just has to change the 4 steps of building habit in a different direction than making it easier. The cue
  should be invisible, the craving should be repealing, the response to be hard and the reward to be unsatisfying.
